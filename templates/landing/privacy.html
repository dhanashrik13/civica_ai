Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

1. Data Collection

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, report a civic issue, or communicate with us. This includes:

  • Name and contact information (email, phone number).
  • Geographical location data when reporting issues.
  • Photos and descriptions of civic issues submitted.
  • Device information and IP addresses.

2. Usage of Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Route civic issues to the appropriate government departments.
  • Provide updates on the status of reported issues.
  • Improve our AI-driven routing and assistance systems.
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our platform.

3. Security

We implement enterprise-grade security measures to protect your data. All sensitive information is encrypted both at rest and in transit. We regularly audit our systems to ensure compliance with the highest security standards.

4. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal information.
  • Opt-out of certain data collection practices.

If you have any questions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@civica-ai.com
